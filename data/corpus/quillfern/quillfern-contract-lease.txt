## Article 1 – Premises
The landlord, Belvedere Fictive Properties, lets to the tenant offices on floor 4 of the Belvedere Residence.

## Article 2 – Rent
The monthly rent is EUR 3,180.00 excluding service charges, payable in advance on the first day of each month.

## Article 3 – Deposit
The tenant pays a security deposit equal to 2 months of rent.

## Article 4 – Indexation
The rent is indexed once a year on the consumer price index.
