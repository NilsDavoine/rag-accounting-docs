## Step 1 – Bank reconciliations
Bank reconciliations are completed within 2 days working days after month end.

## Step 2 – Accruals
Accruals and deferred income are booked before reporting.

## Step 3 – Review
The head of accounting reviews the trial balance and signs off the close.
