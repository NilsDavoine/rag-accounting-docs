## Article 1 – Purpose
The provider Ashcombe Advisory Ltd supports Quillfern Software S.A. with bookkeeping and the preparation of closings.

## Article 2 – Term
The agreement takes effect on 1 March 2025 for a term of 12 months, renewable in writing.

## Article 3 – Fees
Services are invoiced at an hourly rate of EUR 31.00 excluding VAT, based on hours actually worked.

## Article 4 – Payment terms
Invoices are payable within 45 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer.

## Article 5 – Termination
Either party may terminate the agreement with 3 months' notice, given by registered letter.
