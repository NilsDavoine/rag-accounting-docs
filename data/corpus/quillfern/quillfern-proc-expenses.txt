## Step 1 – Submission
Employees submit their expense claim within 15 days of the expense, with receipts attached.

## Step 2 – Approval
The line manager approves the claim. Above EUR 750.00, the CFO's approval is required.

## Step 3 – Reimbursement
Accounting reimburses approved claims within 14 days.

## Step 4 – Archiving
Receipts are kept for 10 years.
